# What's new - 6 October 2026

Gabriel's list from the play-test, plus two new games and an upgrade pack.

**His fixes**
- The axe is now a **Fire axe**: red handle, steel head with a pick, flames licking off the blade.
- **Hat brim**: a "Brim right ▶ / ◀ Brim left" button in the Hat row turns the hat round (cap, cowboy, wizard tip, headband tails all flip). Saved with the hero.
- **Space Quest**: the hero now has a soft glow and a halo around it so it never disappears into the sky, light or dark, on any planet.
- **Monster Brawl controls**: a bigger two-row pad (Jump on top, Punch / Kick / Block underneath, all labelled), the hero turns toward the nearest monster by itself, and taps during a hit no longer get lost (they are kept and fire as soon as the move finishes).
- **No more zoom-in on taps**: double-tap zoom and pinch zoom are switched off across the whole page, so the screen stays put while playing.
- **Venus acid rain** is gentler: fewer, slower, bigger drops, each with a shadow on the ground showing where it will land.
- **Bigger lettering** everywhere: buttons, hints, score bar, game messages, result screens and all text drawn in the games are 15 to 25 percent larger.
- **Endless Dash (and Jump & Run)**: land on top of an alien to squish it like Mario (bounce, star, squish animation). Walking into one still costs a heart.

**Two new games**
- **✈️ Sky Pilot**: your hero flies a yellow plane. Hold ⬆ / ⬇ (or tap the picture to climb), fly through 15 rings, fire 💥 at balloons and birds for bonus points, dodge the storm clouds. Three hearts; land on the runway to win.
- **🏀 Hoop Shot**: 60 seconds on the court. Walk with ◀ ▶, hold 🏀 to charge, let go in the green part of the meter for a swish. Behind the line counts 3. Best score is saved.

**Upgrade pack (look better)**
- New hats: Pirate, Cowboy, Viking, Space helmet, Top hat, Ninja headband.
- New capes: Fire (animated flames), Ice, Galaxy (with stars).
- New tops: Space suit, Pirate coat, Hoodie, Tuxedo, Lava armor.
- New weapons: Laser sword (glows), Katana, Trident, Scythe, Bazooka (shoots rockets). Each has its own reach, damage and speed in fights.
- New **Skin** row: Robot, Zombie, Gold, Ice, Lava, Galaxy, Ghost, Alien. Skins recolour the morphed body (picking one turns Morph on).
- Two new computer fighters: **Lava Lord** and **Astro Ace**. Ninja Nib now carries a katana.

Developer: `node test/run.js` runs 41 checks (was 26).

## Added 7 October - player names and leaderboards

- The first time the game opens it asks for a **player name** (made-up, 3 to 12 letters or numbers, with a 🎲 button for ideas). The 👤 button at the top changes it later. "Skip for now" keeps scores on the device only.
- **Hold any game button for about a second** and that game's **Top 10 leaderboard** pops up, with your row highlighted and your best score underneath. A quick tap still starts the game.
- Every finished game sends its best score. Offline, the board shows the Top 10 from the last time it was online (and how long ago), and new scores wait in a queue until the connection is back.
- Fourteen boards: all eight mini games, Monster Brawl, VS Computer wins, Space Quest fuel cells, House Hunt best time (fastest wins), Sky Pilot and Hoop Shot.
- The online part needs one free Firebase database and a one-line paste into index.html. Steps are in `LEADERBOARD-SETUP.md`. Until then it runs in local-only mode.
