# What's new - 2026-10-29

Full-screen play, finger steering and finger mode (no buttons on touch screens), Monster Pals play buttons, instruction pills that fade, and a QA swarm pass over every game. Commits c5e71aa..7cb4c94.

## Full-screen play, finger steering, Monster Pals play buttons

Game screens now fill the whole window: the page becomes one screen while
playing (top bar, picture, buttons), the picture grows to the full width or
height, and the control buttons float over its bottom edge so the picture can
be as big as the screen. In portrait the buttons park right under the picture.
The browser is also asked for real full screen on game start (Safari hides its
bars) and released on Back.

Finger control on the picture for games that had buttons only: drag left or
right to move (up and down in Sky Pilot), flick up to jump, tap for the action
button, hold still to charge (Hoop Shot). A second finger always taps. Not used
in 2-player or in games that already play by touch. Buttons still work.

Monster Pals: one big PLAY button and colourful place cards in the menu; Copy
Dance buttons are bigger, coloured and labelled, flash when tapped, show the
sequence as you tap it and say "Watch my moves!" first; Snack Toss and
Peekaboo show a tap hint.

## Phone sideways: picture uses the full height, top bar floats as corner pills

On screens 520px tall or less (a phone turned sideways) the top bar no longer
takes its own strip: Back sits top-left, hearts/score/speed/music/sound sit
top-right as small pills over the picture, and fitStage() sizes the picture to
the whole window height. The move buttons land in the dark side bars.

Narrow phones upright: the right button cluster wraps (Jump / Punch Kick /
Block) instead of pushing Block off the right edge.

## Finger mode: fewer buttons, more finger

On touch screens, when every action of a game can be done on the picture,
the on-screen buttons hide and the picture gets the whole screen. A small
"🎮 Buttons" toggle (bottom right) brings them back and is remembered.
A one-line gesture hint is added to the instructions pill at game start.

New gestures: hold still = block (fights) or shield (Space Quest), swipe down
= kick. Existing: drag = move (up/down in Sky Pilot), flick up = jump, tap =
jump/punch/fire, hold = charge in Hoop Shot. Beat Run (tap = jump) and Hero
Kart (hold a side of the picture) already played by finger, so their pads
hide too. 2-player fights keep their pads. Keyboard unchanged.
