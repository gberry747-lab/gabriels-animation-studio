# What's new - 12 Oct 2026

**It's an app now.** Open the game in Safari on the iPad, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open it once online; after that it works with no internet. (The online Top 10 and Online VS still need a connection.)

**Slingshot Siege** - a big new game with 60 levels.
- Fling your own hero from a slingshot at monster towers. Knock them all out to clear the level.
- 5 worlds: Backyard, Castle, Jungle, Moon (low gravity!) and Volcano (TNT!). Each world ends with a 👑 boss fort, and beating it opens the next world.
- Use fewer heroes to earn 3 stars. Stars unlock powers: 💣 Boom, ⚡ Dash, 🦘 Hop and 🪨 Heavy. Tap the screen in mid-air to use one.
- Your best total of stars goes on the Top 10 leaderboard.

**Backyard Defense** - another big new game, 30 levels.
- Slimes march on the clubhouse. Place guards in the lanes to stop them. Every guard is YOUR hero in different gear: Power Pal, Archer, Shield Guard, Knight, Frost Wizard, Hammer Hero and Blaster Ace.
- 5 places: Backyard, Park, Beach, Snow Fort and Night Camp, each ending with a 👑 King Slime.

**Coins and the Gear Shop**
- Every game now pays coins. Spend them in the 🛒 Gear Shop on 17 new items: Dragon and Diamond skins, a Fire sword, Flame hair, a Unicorn horn and more. All the old gear is still free.

**Five more big games** (all in the new "Big adventures" section of Games)
- 🏎️ **Hero Kart**: drive over endless hills, grab battery cells and coins, and upgrade your kart in the Garage. 5 roads.
- 💎 **Gem Quest**: match-3 with Line, Bomb and Star Stones and a Hero Blast. 30 levels.
- 🎵 **Beat Run**: jump to the beat, tap for slimes and hold for big jumps. 18 songs.
- ✏️ **Draw Path**: draw ramps and bridges to roll your hero to a lost pet. 24 puzzles.
- 🐾 **Monster Pals**: make friends with 25 slime pals and pick a Buddy for bonus coins.

**Endless Dash upgrades** in the Gear Shop: Rocket Start, Bubble Shield, Star Magnet and Jetpack.

## Added 13 October - a more exciting Draw tab

- Every drawing tool is a colourful button with a picture: ✏️ Pen, 🧽 Eraser, ↩️ Undo, 🗑️ Clear, 🖼️ Scene.
- **🌈 Rainbow pen:** the rainbow circle at the end of the colours makes every new line a different colour.
- **✨ Sparkle:** turn it on and the pen leaves a trail of glitter stars while you draw.
- **💡 Idea!:** tap it for something to draw, like a ninja with a giant sword or a robot with laser eyes.
- The colour circles are bigger and pop up when picked.

## Added 13 October - a more exciting Games tab

- Every game is a bright card with a big picture on top and its own colour, and a shine sweeps across the cards now and then.
- The three groups have coloured banners: 🏆 Big Adventures, ⚡ Quick Games, ⚔️ Battles & Quests.
- **🎲 Surprise game!** next to the Gear Shop picks a random game and starts it.
- Holding a card still shows its Top 10 leaderboard.

## Added 13 October - Gear Up and the tab buttons

- **Each tab button has its own colour** (Draw blue, Power Moves orange, Gear Up purple, Games green) and pops with a gold glow when picked.
- **Gear Up:** each row has a coloured badge (🎩 Hats, 🦸 Capes, 👕 Tops, ⚔️ Weapons, 🎨 Skins), the gear you are wearing lights up in that colour, and stars burst around your hero when you put something on.
- **Hero Power meter:** ⚔️ Attack, 🛡️ Defense, ⚡ Speed and 🦘 Jump bars that change with your gear, plus a Power level from Rookie up to 🔥 Legendary. The bars match how the gear really works in the fights (a hammer hits hardest, armour and shields protect, ninja gear is fastest, capes jump higher).
- **🎲 Mystery Gear** puts a random outfit on your hero from the gear you own.

## Added 13 October - more exciting game screens

- The bar above every game is a bright purple-to-pink stripe, with the hearts and score in white bubbles. The score bounces when it changes, and the hearts shake when you lose one.
- A big **GO!** (or LET'S GO! / READY? GO!) zooms onto the screen when a game starts.
- The control buttons are coloured by what they do: arrows blue, Jump green, Punch and Fire red, Kick orange, Block and Shield aqua, Down purple. A button glows gold while you hold it.
- **Win screen:** confetti rains down, a trophy, medal, crown or star pops in, and the title turns gold. **Try-again screen:** a 💪 or 🙌 pops in to cheer you on.
